Helping Engineering Students Build Real Things

Microcontrollers Hub was created with a simple goal — make embedded systems learning accessible, practical, and enjoyable for every engineering student, regardless of their background or experience level.

We combine clear theory with hands-on projects. Every concept is backed by working code, circuit diagrams, and step-by-step explanations. No fluff, no paywalls — just solid engineering knowledge.

  • Structured content from absolute beginner to advanced level
  • Real working code for every project and tutorial
  • Covers Arduino, ESP32, PIC, and Raspberry Pi platforms
  • Free datasheets and software download links
  • Mobile-friendly — learn anywhere, anytime
  • Regularly updated with new projects and tutorials
