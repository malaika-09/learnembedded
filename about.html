Our Platform

What is LearnEmbedded?

LearnEmbedded is a free, community-driven educational platform built for students, hobbyists, and engineers who want to master embedded systems — from blinking their first LED to designing complex IoT solutions.

We cover a broad spectrum of microcontroller boards including Arduino, ESP32, PIC, and Raspberry Pi, providing step-by-step tutorials, project guides, component references, and curated resources — all in one place.

Our commitment is simple: no paywalls, no subscriptions. Every tutorial, schematic, and code snippet is freely available because we believe great education should be accessible to everyone, everywhere.

Our Mission

Why We Built This

Three simple pillars guide everything we create and share on this platform.

Educate

We provide clear, well-structured tutorials and project guides that make embedded systems approachable for beginners and valuable for advanced learners.

Empower

We give you the tools, code, schematics, and confidence to turn ideas into working hardware — removing barriers between imagination and creation.

Inspire

We showcase real-world projects and innovations to spark curiosity and show what's possible when creativity meets engineering knowledge.

Our Values

What We Stand For

These principles shape every piece of content and every decision we make.

🎯

Accuracy

Every schematic, code example, and technical explanation is reviewed for correctness. We don't publish content we haven't tested ourselves.

🔓

Accessibility

All content is free, jargon is explained, and resources are structured for learners at every level — from complete beginners to seasoned makers.

🔧

Practicality

We focus on real, buildable projects and applied knowledge — not just theory. If you can't put it on a breadboard, it doesn't belong here.

🤝

Community

We build together. Every learner, contributor, and tinkerer is welcome. Sharing knowledge multiplies its value for everyone.
